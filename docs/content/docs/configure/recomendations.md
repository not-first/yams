# What should YOU do now?

Everything is working now! Yay! 🎉

Here are some cool ways to make your YAMS experience even better:

### Learn
You want to be in control of your server, and hence you must understand it too. If you are unsure about the role of any application, do some personal research about how it works. I know its a touchy topic within this community, but AI can be quite handy for asking specific questions like this, or explaining how anything within your YAMS compose works! 🙃

Check out the [fundamentals](/docs/fundamentals/docker-and-compose/) of the YAMS website if you haven't already to learn the basics about Docker, Torrenting, and how environment variables and hardlinks work within YAMS.

### Add more indexers! 🔍
Learn about [torrent indexers](/docs/fundamentals/torrenting-and-vpns/#trackers) (also known as indexers). The more indexers you have, the easier it'll be to find those hard-to-get movies, and the higher chance your Radarr/Sonarr can source a higher quality release. Each tracker has its own community, different rules, and differing requirements of entry (especially private trackers). Finding a popular one with reliable seeding and a wide spread of media can be great for starting out, and might even be all you need.

### Get those apps! 📱
Install your media service apps (Emby/Jellyfin/Plex) on all your devices.

Generally they're available for:
- Laptops (Linux, macOS, Windows)
- Phones (Android, iOS)
- TVs (Android, Google, Amazon Fire, Chromecast, Roku, Xbox)
- Home Theater PCs
- Nintendo 3DS ⁉️
- And lots more!
